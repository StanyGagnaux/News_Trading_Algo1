## 2. H1 restated: "no tradable drift"

Replaces the two-sided 5% test proposed for H1 in the original configuration.

For each scorer (keyword, LLM) and each horizon (day 1, 2, 3, 5) — 8 cells —
take the mean market-adjusted signed return from entry to the close of day k
and its date-clustered standard error (event-study specification, section 4
of the original configuration). Cell i tests
H0: mean ≥ 4.86 bps (the round-trip cost) against mean < 4.86 bps, with
p_i = Φ((mean_i − 4.86) / se_i) (one-sided, normal approximation).
Holm correction across the 8 cells at 5%: order the p-values ascending and
reject while p_(k) ≤ 0.05 / (8 − k + 1).

- **H1 holds for a cell** if its H0 is rejected, i.e. the Holm-corrected
  one-sided 95% upper confidence bound lies below 4.86 bps.
- Cells where it is not rejected (the bound reaches or exceeds the cost) are
  reported as **inconclusive**, not as evidence of drift.
