"For LLM signals in the holdout, the market-adjusted signed return from entry
to the close of day 3 increases with |strength|."

## Test (single evaluation)

- Sample: every LLM holdout event of the frozen event-study specification
  (`FROZEN_CONFIG_2026-10-18.md` section 4; daily gated signals, Bug 11 rule),
  entry dates 2026-06-02 → 2026-10-16, Yahoo adjusted OHLC and ^NDX as in
  `FROZEN_CONFIG_2026-10-18_note_2026-10-18.md` item 4.
- y = market-adjusted signed return from the entry open to the close of day 3
  (bps); x = |net daily strength| of the event.
- Estimator: OLS y = a + b·x; standard error of b clustered by entry date
  (CR1: sandwich × G/(G−1)) — `src/h3.py`, function `h3_decision`.
- Decision: one-sided at 5%; H3 supported if t = b / SE > 1.645.
- Evaluated once; outside the H1 Holm family and separate from H2.
