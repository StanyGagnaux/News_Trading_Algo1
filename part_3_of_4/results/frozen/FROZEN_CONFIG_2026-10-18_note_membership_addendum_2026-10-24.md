  **also favours the momentum benchmark, likely more than the news
  strategies**: momentum buys recent winners, and a universe chosen with
  hindsight from firms that later joined the index over-represents stocks

  hold for 1–3 days, so they are less exposed to this persistence.

  a point-in-time universe, so "no news strategy beats momentum" is stated
  with this caveat; the bias cannot be sized without membership history.
